# BI certifier 4.0: mathematical review

This is a reviewed executable certificate argument, not a proof-assistant formalization. Numerical BI convergence is never a proof premise. This release keeps the two endpoint certificate methods from numeric v2/v3 and makes their common proof check explicit.

## Contract and proof boundary

Input is a connected polygonal sphere with regular unit-side polygons of sizes 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 or 10. Vertices have strictly positive intrinsic curvature. Different input faces intersect in at most one vertex or one common boundary edge. Neighboring input faces may be coplanar in space. Full-dimensional convex endpoint realizations are supported; a doubled planar polygon is not certified by either endpoint method.

The producer, its floating-point BI estimates, symmetry reduction, numerical Newton refinement, Qhull proposals and all resource limits are untrusted search aids. Every accepted result is independently replayed by `python -S check.py`, using only standard-library exact integer/rational arithmetic. Failed search or a failed sufficient certificate condition is unresolved. An invalid certificate cannot exclude a candidate.

The underlying existence/uniqueness theorem is Bobenko–Izmestiev, *Alexandrov's theorem, weighted Delaunay triangulations, and mixed volumes*, Theorem 1.1 and Definition 1.2: [primary paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.2358.pdf), printed p. 448. It also describes the doubled-polygon limit and why intrinsic input edges need not be hull edges. The new code does not claim a certified trajectory for either their deformation or the numerical gradient optimizer.

## Obligations checked by the implementation

1. **Input metric.** `metric.validate_input` checks face sizes, simple cycles, unique face vertex sets and contiguous integer labels. `metric.setup` checks pair intersections, two faces per original edge and angle sum below 2π by rational arithmetic. The triangulated complex has a single connected cycle at each vertex, two triangles per edge, connected underlying graph and Euler characteristic two. These conditions identify a closed connected spherical surface. Triangle labels, duplicates and the base triangle are explicitly validated.
2. **Intrinsic marking.** `initial_metric` constructs regular-polygon fan lengths from exact chord constants. `field.chord_squared` evaluates the cosine recurrence in Q(√2,√3,√5), with the positive real square-root embedding. Each recorded flip is replayed across its two actual adjacent triangles. The developed quadrilateral is proved strictly convex by positive heights and an interior diagonal intersection. The new diagonal therefore preserves the marked intrinsic metric. Parallel intrinsic edges remain unsupported and lead to an unresolved route, not rejection of the body. The final supplied triangles must equal the reconstructed complex.
3. **Arithmetic.** Endpoints are integers divided by 2^bits. Addition/subtraction are exact; products/divisions use integer floor/ceiling, with denominators separated from zero. Integer square roots are rounded outward. Rational input is parsed before interval construction; floats and booleans in certificate rational fields are rejected. Reversed rational intervals are rejected before rounding. Precision is process-global, so parallel proof replays use separate processes.
4. **Root existence.** `proof.contraction_image` is the single implementation of the square Banach/Krawczyk argument. It bounds M=I−YJ(X), proves ||M||∞<1, and strict inclusion of c−YF(c)+M(X−c) in X. Hence H(x)=x−YF(x) contracts X into itself. The norm bound makes Y nonsingular: otherwise every YA would be singular and I−YA would have eigenvalue one. The unique fixed point is a root. For nondyadic rational centers the stored interval encloses the exact proposed center; all residual, displacement and image bounds cover that center. Exact intrinsic constants may be enclosed intervals; they are fixed true constants, not free equation variables.
5. **Strict endpoint.** `check.certify_metric_root` fixes the three base vertices using exact intrinsic lengths, leaving 3V−9 variables and equations. `convex_support` then proves distinct vertices, nondegenerate triangles, a coherent orientation and strictly supporting planes against every other vertex. The checked triangulated sphere consists of exposed hull facets and exhausts the hull boundary. A zero-containing support interval never proves weak convexity.
6. **Coplanar endpoint.** `hull_metric.prepare` checks a coarse polygonal sphere, all labeled vertices on that graph, degree at least three, proper patch intersections and complete triangulations of each patch. `verify_geometry` proves a strict center hull, full dimension throughout X, exposure of every vertex, and exposure of every coarse edge by an identically perpendicular supporting vector. Thus the coarse graph stays embedded with its rotation system and patch disks throughout the box. All possible hull refinements of each disk occur in the Catalan list. `functions` develops every such triangulation and proves a simple strictly convex developed polygon by oriented edge supports. Its interval derivatives cover every piece of the marked intrinsic hull metric. Formulas agree when a hull diagonal changes through a planar face. A finite continuous piecewise-C1 map obeys the interval mean-value bound on segments, including segments in transition sets by continuity. The common contraction proof therefore applies. No guessed zero dihedral is treated as a proved equality. See `HULL_METRIC_PROOF.md` for the detailed argument.
7. **Original-face exclusion.** At the certified convex endpoint, an original regular flat face must have every prescribed squared chord and zero heights relative to its first three noncollinear vertices. A rigorously separated chord or height defect therefore excludes that original face realization. Alexandrov uniqueness identifies the marked convex metric realization up to rigid motion. Checking heights alone would miss some folded triangular disks; all-pairs chord witnesses remain in the checker. If no defect is separated from zero, the result is OPEN_FOR_ALGEBRAIC. Exact original-face flatness and CRP° membership are never asserted.

## Changes and why they preserve the proof

- All proof `assert` statements became explicit `require` checks. They remain active under Python `-O`. No inequality was weakened.
- Both certificate routes call the same contraction routine. The Jacobian remains sparse, but every matrix entry is bounded. No symmetry is used to reduce the verified equation system.
- Dot products sum integer minima/maxima of endpoint products, then round once. For interval factors [a,b], [c,d], the extrema of their product are among ac, ad, bc, bd. Summing these extrema with common denominator 2^(2 bits) gives an outward enclosure, at least as tight as separately rounded summands. This is not floating-point accumulation.
- Integer interval endpoints use multiplication by 2^bits directly, avoiding Fraction construction. This is exact, including negative and arbitrarily large integers; rational inputs retain directed rounding.
- Exact chord constants are cached; their interval conversion is performed at the current precision. No interval is reused after a precision change. An unused all-pairs length table and unused dense Jacobian were removed.
- Internal fold-angle calculation, its π enclosure and inverse-cosine bisection were removed from verdict production. Chord/height witnesses suffice for the eight existing exclusions. This only removes possible sufficient rejection witnesses; it cannot create a false rejection. Existing certificate schema inputs remain readable, and report fields for internal folds remain present as empty compatibility fields.
- Proposal-internal duplicate replays were removed. Each route is still verified before selection, and the launcher still independently replays the selected certificate. Producer verdict flags cannot authorize exclusion.
- Symmetry enumeration maps a directed seed-face edge to every possible target of the same face size, with each orientation sign, then propagates across uniquely paired face edges. Any automorphism has exactly one such seed image and sign, and adjacency forces all subsequent images. Global vertex bijectivity and consistent face propagation are checked. Its complete permutations and orbits were compared with v3's independent graph matcher on all 206 inputs. Symmetry is only a numerical heuristic, so even a missed symmetry could not validate an incorrect proof.

## Findings and limits

No unsound acceptance or exclusion rule was identified in the reviewed valid-input endpoint arguments. The removed optional angle code had a larger arithmetic/proof surface than needed for this census. Explicit checks improve resistance to malformed certificates and accidental use of optimization mode; the older CLI already refused `-O`, so this is not evidence that its recorded proofs bypassed checks.

There is no general completeness guarantee for certificate discovery, no proof of gradient convergence, and no exact-flatness acceptance in BI. Complete Catalan enumeration and its cap of 2,000 triangulations per patch are retained. Exceeding that cap is an unresolved certificate attempt. Noninterior centers or failed numerical starts cannot imply nonexistence. The finite face menu is part of this implementation's input contract, not a theorem that it covers every possible definition of CRP°.

The saved replay and fresh-run evidence are reported separately in `VALIDATION.md`. Passing tests and agreement with earlier results support the implementation but do not substitute for the mathematical argument above.
